THE STORY

A FRIENDSHIP
WORTH REMEMBERING

Best friends Jo and Raissa have shared countless memories throughout their lives, but as they prepare to follow separate paths, they face the possibility of growing apart. During their last night together, an unexpected discovery leads them through a mysterious portal to Nakali, a magical island inspired by Filipino folklore.

In this unfamiliar world, the two friends encounter extraordinary creatures, mysterious places, and unexpected challenges unlike anything they have experienced before. As they search for a way back home, their journey becomes more than just an adventure. Their friendship, trust, and the precious memories they share are put to the test.

With the dangers of Nakali surrounding them, Jo and Raissa must learn to face the unknown together and hold on to what matters most. Because sometimes, the greatest adventure is discovering just how far friendship can take you.
